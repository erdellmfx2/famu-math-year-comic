# The Formula of Becoming: X Launch Campaign

Campaign window: **July 29–August 9, 2026 (EDT)**
Primary platform: **X**
Primary outcome: launch on July 31 and turn the first ten daily episodes into the opening of a reliable reader habit.

## Campaign Position

**The Formula of Becoming** is a daily, G-rated modern comic about Malik Baptiste and Nia Reynolds, two first-year mathematics students at the fictional McCall-Hart University. It makes math matter because the characters have real choices to make: relationships, family pressure, campus life, competition, and work that can help a community.

The campaign should feel inviting, emotionally honest, and specific. It should not market the series as generic study motivation, turn romance into a prize, or imply that McCall-Hart is a real institution.

### Who it is for

- College-age readers and young adults who enjoy character-driven serialized stories.
- Readers drawn to HBCU-inspired campus culture, mathematics, research, and sports stories.
- Mathematics educators and students who want to share a human story about quantitative work.

### Core message

> Becoming is not the same as having every answer.

### Campaign tags

Use **#TheFormulaOfBecoming** on every campaign post. Add only one topical tag when useful: **#CollegeComics**, **#MathStories**, or **#Webcomic**. Avoid tag clusters.

## Publication Rule

The active story is set at **fictional McCall-Hart University**. Public campaign
materials may use the neutral credit **“Presented by the Mathematics Department.”**
Do not use a real-university name, mark, building, color identity, or claim that
McCall-Hart's fictional people and events are real. Several older files still contain
legacy FAMU naming or marks; do not publish those as-is.

For each episode post, use one compelling lettered page or a short captioned motion-comic clip. Keep the main post to one asset when possible; add a second panel only when the first ends on a clear story question.

## Daily Community Routine

1. Post the episode at the planned time, then stay available for 20–30 minutes.
2. Reply first to comments that make a specific observation or answer the question in the post.
3. Like thoughtful reader replies; do not reply with a generic thank-you to every comment.
4. Within 24 hours, quote-post one strong non-spoiler reader response only with the reader's permission if it contains personal detail.
5. Log impressions, engagement rate, replies, reposts, video completion (where available), and profile visits in the campaign CSV.

## Weekly Content Mix After Launch

Keep the daily episode as the anchor. Add only two supporting posts each week so the feed remains story-first:

| Day | Supporting post | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friday evening | “Next week” teaser | Build anticipation without revealing the resolution. |
| Sunday evening | Reader question or a one-panel process note | Invite conversation and give new followers an entry point. |

Rotate these four supporting angles: character observation, a mathematical idea in plain language, a place on campus, and a behind-the-scenes lettering/voiceover note. When sharing a motion comic, disclose: **“Voiceovers in this clip use AI-generated voices.”**

## Practical X Workflow

1. On x.com or in the X app, select the compose/Post button.
2. Paste one calendar caption and attach the approved image(s) or one captioned video.
3. Add concise alt text to every image. Describe the story action, characters, setting, and any essential readable text; do not write “image of.”
4. Confirm that the post has no FAMU/real-university identity, no accidental spoiler, and no placeholder link.
5. Select **Post** to publish immediately, or select the calendar icon in the compose box to choose a date and time, then confirm the scheduled post.
6. Check that the sent/scheduled post contains the intended media and correct reading order. Keep the launch post pinned.

X's current help says a standard post can contain up to four media items and 280 characters. It also supports scheduling from the compose box; drafts and scheduled posts are available under **Unsent posts**. See [X Help: How to post](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/how-to-post). For multi-post explanations or recaps, use the compose plus control to create a thread; see [X Help: Create a thread](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/create-a-thread).

## Success Check at the End of August 9

This opening campaign has succeeded if it establishes a repeatable release habit and gives the next week a stronger start. Review:

- Which episode received the highest replies, reposts, and profile visits.
- Whether video posts earned more meaningful replies than still panels.
- Which reader question generated the most answers.
- Whether every public asset honored the fictional McCall-Hart identity.
- Whether the audience can state the premise, recognize Malik and Nia, and know when the next episode arrives.

Use those findings to set Week 3's time, format, and discussion question. Do not change the series voice merely to chase a single high-impression post.
