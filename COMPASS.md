# Project Compass: *The Formula of Becoming*

> **Read this first.** This is the strategic north star for all active work in this project.

## North Star

Create and share a daily, character-led mathematics comic that makes quantitative work feel human, rigorous, and connected to students' lives. *The Formula of Becoming* follows Malik Baptiste and Nia Reynolds through relationships, family pressure, campus culture, academic challenges, and community-facing research at the **fictional** McCall-Hart University.

The work serves readers who want a compelling college story, students who need to see mathematics as lived practice, and educators/outreach partners who need honest, accessible material for conversation about mathematical thinking. McCall-Hart is not a real institution and must never be represented as one.

## Near-Term Goals

1. Maintain an identity-compliant public package: McCall-Hart names, indigo/copper/cream visual language, and no legacy FAMU marks, venues, colors, or institutional claims in active releases.
2. Produce approved episodes through a sustainable weekly rhythm: authored story, reviewed art, clear lettering, accessible media, and complete release packages.
3. Launch and sustain the X campaign with reliable episode posts, a clear series premise, reader questions, and a routine for responding to audience engagement.
4. Build a repeatable handoff system so comics, captioned motion pieces, captions, alt text, disclosures, and posting notes are ready before publication.

## Long-Term Goals

1. Complete the 54-week, 374-episode story without reducing its academic, emotional, or community stakes.
2. Sustain a coherent fictional McCall-Hart world in which Calculus I–III, research, athletics, family, friendship, and romance affect one another.
3. Grow an audience for character-led mathematics storytelling and make participation easy for students, readers, and educators.
4. Create reusable, accessible outreach materials—selected comic sequences, discussion prompts, and transparent process notes—that invite meaningful conversation about mathematics and college life.
5. Preserve a reliable, documented production archive that supports future adaptations without confusing active v2 work with historical material.

## Non-Negotiables

- **Fictional setting:** Active work is set at McCall-Hart University in Bellwether, Alabama. Archived FAMU-based material is historical reference only.
- **Mathematical integrity:** Mathematics must be specific, accurate, legible when it matters, and integrated into actual choices or consequences.
- **Emotional integrity:** Characters have agency. No romance is a prize, no rival is a villain by default, and failures remain meaningful.
- **Audience care:** Keep the series G-rated, accessible, and emotionally honest. Use alt text for images, captions for spoken media, and disclose AI-generated voiceovers.
- **Canon and approval:** Create new storyboards, art, or publication assets only from approved active v2 story material. Do not bypass the approval gate.
- **Sustainable release:** Protect the production budget and use the documented handoff/review process rather than improvising public releases.

## Decision Filter

Before approving an idea, asset, post, partnership, or workflow change, ask:

1. Does it strengthen the story, mathematical integrity, or reader connection?
2. Is it faithful to fictional McCall-Hart identity and the active v2 canon?
3. Does it preserve character agency, G-rated tone, and accessibility?
4. Is it approved, sustainable, and ready to share without creating a misleading institutional claim?

If any answer is no or uncertain, pause and resolve the conflict before proceeding.
