# Story Bible v2

## Working Title

**The Formula of Becoming**

## Setting

McCall-Hart University, a fictional HBCU in the fictional river city of Bellwether, Alabama. School colors are indigo, copper, and cream. The Herons compete in the fictional Southern Collegiate Heritage Conference.

McCall-Hart is not Florida A&M University under another name. See `story/setting_bible_v2.md` for the full identity and source boundary.

## Protagonists

- Malik Baptiste, first-year Actuarial Science major from Gulfport, Mississippi
- Nia Reynolds, first-year Mathematical Sciences major from Huntsville, Alabama

See `story/character_bible_v2.md` for supporting characters, family, faculty, voice, visual direction, and continuity rules.

## Timeframe

August 1, 2025 through August 9, 2026, covering Summer Bridge, fall, winter break, spring, and a full summer sequence.

## Tone

Warm, funny, emotionally honest, G-rated, academically grounded, and specific to HBCU campus life. The story allows disappointment, jealousy, family strain, heartbreak, and failure without becoming cynical.

## Long Arcs

- Nia and Julian's fall relationship and breakup
- Malik's unspoken fall hope for Nia
- Malik's family home loss and his father's injury after a Gulf storm
- Malik's unsuccessful fall Heron Analytics Challenge
- Malik and Simone's spring relationship and breakup
- Nia's unspoken spring hope for Malik
- Nia's unsuccessful spring fellowship pursuit
- DJ's football breakthrough and scholarship increase
- Imani's strong softball season and tournament-semifinal loss
- Keisha's track and research-software authorship
- The evacuation-model project, conferences, presentations, and Delta National Laboratory
- Malik and Nia's summer relationship and second-year outlook

## Academic Spine

- Fall Calculus I: limits, continuity, derivatives, applications, antiderivatives, definite integrals, and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
- Spring Calculus II: integration applications and techniques, improper integrals, sequences and series, convergence, power series, parametric curves, and polar coordinates
- Summer Calculus III: vectors, multivariable differentiation, optimization, multiple integrals, vector fields, and integral theorems

## Story Rule

Every episode requires a present want, an obstacle, a choice, and a changed condition. Mathematics and events must affect plot rather than decorate it.
