# Voice Casting: Darius "DJ" Cole

**Base voice:** `echo`  
**Age:** 18  
**Function:** Malik's roommate, football player, and statistics-curious friend.

## Performance Direction

Energetic, socially easy, and quick with affectionate commentary. Use a grounded athletic voice with a lively medium-fast pace, but keep him thoughtful when discussing film study, family pressure, playing time, or scholarship money. His jokes build connection rather than hiding a lack of depth. After setbacks, lower the energy instead of turning the scene into a speech.

## Continuity

- Default pace: Medium-fast.
- Roommate mode: Playful, spontaneous, expressive.
- Athlete mode: Focused, practical, alert.
- Stress tell: Jokes once, then becomes unexpectedly plainspoken.
- Avoid: Loudness as personality, locker-room stereotype, and comic-relief-only delivery.

> We have not moved in and you are already taking square footage from joy.
