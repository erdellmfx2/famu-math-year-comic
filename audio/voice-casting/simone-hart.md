# Voice Casting: Simone Hart

**Base voice:** `marin`  
**Age:** 19  
**Function:** Data science and finance student, analytics leader, and Malik's spring relationship.

## Performance Direction

Clear, poised, direct, and emotionally intelligent. Use a steady pace and crisp phrasing that communicates preparation without stiffness. She does not soften a necessary truth, but she is not cold. Humor is spare and perceptive. In vulnerable scenes, let certainty give way gradually; her hesitation matters because she usually speaks only after deciding.

## Continuity

- Default pace: Medium.
- Leadership mode: Focused, calm, decisive.
- Relationship mode: Warm but exact; she names what others avoid.
- Stress tell: Delays the conversation until her decision is nearly complete.
- Avoid: Villainous sharpness, corporate jargon, superiority, and icy detachment.

## Audition Line

> Bring that suspicion with you. Leave room for other people to be right.
