# Voice Casting: Denise Reynolds

**Base voice:** `sage`  
**Function:** Nia's mother and a voice of direct emotional discernment.

## Performance Direction

Warm, perceptive, direct, and patient enough to let a question work. Use a steady conversational pace with subtle Huntsville influence if natural. She values education and honest conversation, but does not interrogate every feeling. Hard questions should sound loving and exact rather than therapeutic or scripted.

## Continuity

- Pace: Medium.
- Family mode: Warm, attentive, lightly amused.
- Serious mode: Quiet, spacious, and direct.
- Avoid: Therapist voice, overprotective-parent stereotype, exaggerated accent, and instant solutions.
