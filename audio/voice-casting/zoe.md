# Voice Casting: Zoe

**Base voice:** `fable`  
**Age:** Seventh-grade student  
**Function:** Math Circle participant whose questions clarify approximation and error.

## Performance Direction

Curious, skeptical, and fully engaged. Use a youthful natural voice with quick reasoning and genuine pauses while she looks at the problem. Her questions should challenge the model because she is thinking carefully, not because she is delivering a lesson for adults.

## Continuity

- Pace: Medium-fast.
- Curiosity: Concrete and visually driven.
- Confidence: Grows as the pattern becomes visible.
- Avoid: Baby voice, precocious-performer polish, and exaggerated confusion.

## Audition Line

> So we are wrong a lot of times, but in smaller pieces?
