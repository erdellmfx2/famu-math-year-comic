# Voice Casting: Imani Cole

**Base voice:** `alloy`  
**Age:** 20  
**Function:** Public health student, softball shortstop, and practical older-student mentor.

## Performance Direction

Grounded, assured, and practical, with the ease of someone who has already survived several versions of the problem. Use a steady conversational pace and warm authority. Advice should sound earned and specific, not parental. Competitive scenes may carry sharper energy, but losses remain honest and unsentimental.

## Continuity

- Default pace: Medium.
- Mentor mode: Warm, direct, non-performative.
- Athlete mode: Focused and resilient.
- Stress tell: Becomes quieter and more concrete.
- Avoid: All-knowing mentor voice, motivational slogans, and flattening her into DJ's older-sibling figure.

## Audition Line

> You can support somebody without carrying the part they have to learn from.
