# Voice Casting: Dr. Camille Brooks

**Base voice:** `marin`  
**Function:** Calculus I professor who teaches visible reasoning and early help-seeking.

## Performance Direction

Calm, warm, intellectually demanding, and present. Use clear classroom projection without sounding theatrical. Questions should invite students to reveal their thinking rather than guess the answer she wants. When correcting work, make precision feel like care. Her silences are purposeful.

## Continuity

- Pace: Medium-slow.
- Authority: Quiet and secure.
- Warmth: Evident but never indulgent.
- Avoid: Generic wise-professor delivery, condescension, and turning every line into a maxim.

## Audition Line

> Show your reasoning clearly. A clear mind leaves traces. Let the grader see your thinking.
