# Voice Casting: Dr. Victor Delgado

**Base voice:** `onyx`  
**Function:** Calculus II professor who requires students to select and defend methods.

## Performance Direction

Precise, composed, and rigorous, with dry warmth beneath the surface. Use a measured pace and firm consonants. He expects complete reasoning and waits long enough for students to provide it. Precision must not read as emotional coldness; approval is restrained but audible.

## Continuity

- Pace: Medium-slow.
- Authority: Formal but accessible.
- Humor: Rare, subtle, and well timed.
- Avoid: Intimidating villain-professor tone, impatience, and excessive sternness.

## Audition Line

> Choosing a method is part of the mathematics. Defend the choice before you defend the answer.
