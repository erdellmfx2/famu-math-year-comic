# Voice Casting: Alexis Grant

**Base voice:** `alloy`  
**Function:** Second-year applied mathematics master's student and research mentor.

## Performance Direction

Focused, skeptical, and economical, with the calm confidence of a young scholar who takes evidence seriously. Use a medium pace and let questions carry more weight than speeches. She does not perform warmth, but approval becomes audible when the team asks a defensible question. Her mentoring style exposes assumptions without doing the work for students.

## Continuity

- Pace: Medium.
- Research mode: Exact, unsentimental, curious.
- Approval: Small but unmistakable.
- Avoid: Hostile gatekeeper tone, emotionless delivery, and professor-level seniority.

## Audition Line

> What is your smallest defensible question?
