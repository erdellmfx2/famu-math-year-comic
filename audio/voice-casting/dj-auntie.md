# Voice Casting: DJ's Auntie

**Base voice:** `coral`  
**Function:** Supporting family voice during move-in and game-day scenes.

## Performance Direction

Expansive, affectionate, funny, and completely sincere. Use strong energy and confident projection while keeping the character grounded as a real family member. Her game-day volume is situational; quieter family moments should retain warmth without becoming a performance.

## Continuity

- Pace: Medium-fast.
- Move-in mode: Welcoming, teasing, practical.
- Game-day mode: Joyfully loud and unselfconscious.
- Avoid: Cartoonish volume, exaggerated dialect, and comic-prop treatment.

## Audition Line

> That is my baby!
