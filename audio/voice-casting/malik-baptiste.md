# Voice Casting: Malik Baptiste

**Base voice:** `ash`  
**Age:** 18  
**Function:** Co-lead; first-year actuarial science student from Gulfport, Mississippi.

## Performance Direction

Thoughtful, precise, and economical. Speak in a measured medium-low register with clean diction and small pauses before answers. Dry humor should sound discovered, not performed. As trust grows, allow more warmth and easier rhythm. Under fear or family pressure, become more formal, controlled, and clipped while keeping the feeling audible beneath the polish. Use only a light Gulf Coast influence if it emerges naturally; never caricature it.

## Continuity

- Default pace: Medium-slow.
- Public mode: Composed, concise, slightly guarded.
- Private mode: Softer and warmer, with unfinished thoughts.
- Stress tell: Overprecision and reduced vocal flexibility.
- Avoid: Robotic delivery, constant math cadence, arrogance, and emotional flatness.
