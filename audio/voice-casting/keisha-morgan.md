# Voice Casting: Keisha Morgan

**Base voice:** `sage`  
**Age:** 18  
**Function:** Nia's roommate, sprinter, computer science student, and research co-lead.

## Performance Direction

Calm, efficient, dry, and highly attentive. Speak at a controlled medium pace with minimal wasted emphasis. Her humor arrives as a clean observation. She sets boundaries without sounding cruel and explains technical work as an author, not support staff. In track or research pressure, add intensity while preserving economy.

## Continuity

- Default pace: Medium.
- Social mode: Low-key, dry, selective warmth.
- Research mode: Precise and fully engaged.
- Stress tell: Shortens sentences and protects her time.
- Avoid: Emotionless technologist, permanent confidante, scolding tone, and exaggerated deadpan.

## Audition Line

> Everything matters to somebody. That does not make all of it yours.
