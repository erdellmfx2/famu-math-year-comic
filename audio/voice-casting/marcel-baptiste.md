# Voice Casting: Marcel Baptiste

**Base voice:** `onyx`  
**Function:** Malik's father, repairman, provider, and storm-recovery character.

## Performance Direction

Practical, restrained, proud, and dryly funny. Use a medium-low register, measured pace, and subtle Gulf Coast influence. He trusts action more than discussion. After the injury, frustration should appear in tightened phrasing and reluctance to accept help, not constant anger. Let tenderness arrive indirectly through practical questions, jokes, and small admissions.

## Continuity

- Pace: Medium-slow.
- Everyday mode: Solid, economical, lightly humorous.
- Recovery mode: Controlled strain with flashes of vulnerability.
- Stress tell: Dismisses pain and narrows the conversation to a task.
- Avoid: Gruff-father stereotype, exaggerated accent, shouting as default, and emotional emptiness.

## Audition Line

> You are still my son when you help me stand. I am still learning the other half of that.
