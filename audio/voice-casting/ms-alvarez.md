# Voice Casting: Ms. Alvarez

**Base voice:** `shimmer`  
**Function:** Malik's North Star Learning supervisor.

## Performance Direction

Efficient, observant, and humane. Use a brisk but unhurried workplace cadence. She notices when Malik is overextending himself and responds with practical care instead of a dramatic intervention. Instructions are concise; concern is usually embedded in a task, a schedule adjustment, or one well-placed word.

## Continuity

- Pace: Medium.
- Work mode: Clear, organized, low-drama.
- Care mode: Brief, direct, unmistakable.
- Avoid: Maternal stereotype, bureaucratic coldness, and speechifying.
