# Voice Casting: Micah

**Base voice:** `nova`  
**Age:** 12  
**Function:** Malik's cousin, family witness, and source of curious mathematical questions.

## Performance Direction

Youthful, curious, candid, and emotionally intelligent without sounding adult. Use a light, natural pace with quick follow-up questions and occasional uncertainty. After the storm, allow quieter delivery and longer pauses around loss. His humor comes from sincere logic, not a sitcom-child performance.

## Continuity

- Pace: Medium-fast in play; slower after the storm.
- Curiosity: Direct and specific.
- Emotion: Honest but not precociously polished.
- Avoid: Baby voice, exaggerated cuteness, shrillness, and adult academic phrasing.
