# Voice Casting: Celeste Baptiste

**Base voice:** `shimmer`  
**Function:** Malik's mother and a central voice in the Gulfport family arc.

## Performance Direction

Warm, practical, observant, and emotionally sturdy. Speak with a natural Gulf Coast rhythm that remains subtle and contemporary. She can move from humor to logistics in one breath. During the storm and recovery, let fatigue roughen the edges without erasing care. She recognizes Malik's control strategies and speaks to the person beneath them.

## Continuity

- Pace: Medium.
- Family mode: Warm, teasing, efficient.
- Crisis mode: Direct, tired, and focused.
- Stress tell: Gives concrete information before naming emotion.
- Avoid: Saintly mother voice, melodrama, exaggerated accent, and endless reassurance.

> Your job money helps. But school is still your job.
