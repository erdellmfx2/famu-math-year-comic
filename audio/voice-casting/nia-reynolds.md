# Voice Casting: Nia Reynolds

**Base voice:** `coral`  
**Age:** 18  
**Function:** Co-lead; first-year mathematical sciences student from Huntsville, Alabama.

## Performance Direction

Bright, expressive, curious, and emotionally available. Speak at a lively conversational pace with agile changes in emphasis, as if ideas become examples while she talks. Humor is playful and inviting, never cruel. Slow down when she recognizes a human consequence or finally listens. When overcommitted or afraid of losing belonging, let the words arrive too quickly and slightly ahead of her certainty. Keep any Alabama influence subtle and natural.

## Continuity

- Default pace: Medium-fast.
- Public mode: Open, animated, confident.
- Private mode: Honest, searching, occasionally overexplained.
- Stress tell: Volunteers, reassures, or fills silence too quickly.
- Avoid: Perpetual cheerfulness, breathless delivery on every line, sarcasm, and a generic "spunky" voice.

## Audition Line

> Private is fine. Panicked is different.
