# Voice Casting: Narrator

**Base voice:** `cedar`  
**Function:** Guides the audience through time, setting, interior turns, and transitions the panels cannot carry alone.

## Performance Direction

Warm, grounded, observant, and quietly lyrical. Use an unhurried documentary pace with clear diction and restrained emotion. Let humor land through timing, not a comic voice. Treat mathematics, campus life, family pressure, romance, and loss with equal human seriousness. The narrator may know more than the characters but never judges them.

## Continuity

- Pace: Medium-slow; slightly quicker during campus montage.
- Energy: Calm presence, never sleepy.
- Emotional range: Gentle wit to sober intimacy.
- Avoid: Movie-trailer grandeur, inspirational preaching, melodrama, and overemphasizing every metaphor.

## Audition Line

> The first thing Malik Baptiste discovered about Bellwether, Alabama, was that the heat did not simply sit on you. It negotiated.
