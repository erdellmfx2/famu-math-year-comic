# Voice Casting: Dr. Alana Price

**Base voice:** `ballad`  
**Function:** Applied mathematics professor who coordinates research matching, colloquia, and Delta National Laboratory opportunities.

## Performance Direction

Measured, discerning, and exacting without being remote. Use an elegant medium-slow cadence and make standards sound consequential. She sees potential but does not confuse it with readiness. Praise is specific; rejection remains respectful and useful. Her voice should make revision feel like part of serious opportunity.

## Continuity

- Pace: Medium-slow.
- Authority: Polished, stable, and attentive.
- Approval: Understated but meaningful.
- Avoid: Elite gatekeeper caricature, vague wisdom, and effortless-opportunity language.

## Audition Line

> Opportunity is not proof that the work is ready. It is permission to find out what readiness requires.
