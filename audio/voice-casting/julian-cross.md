# Voice Casting: Julian Cross

**Base voice:** `verse`  
**Age:** 19  
**Function:** Media arts student, saxophonist, campus radio host, and Nia's first serious relationship.

## Performance Direction

Warm, smooth, and publicly confident, with a musician's sense of timing. Radio lines should feel effortless and welcoming without becoming a broadcast caricature. In person, use more texture, self-awareness, and occasional awkward pauses. He listens closely and lets people finish. In vulnerable relationship scenes, reduce the polish so the audience hears a sincere young man rather than a performer.

## Continuity

- Default pace: Medium with rhythmic phrasing.
- On-air mode: Relaxed, buoyant, clean projection.
- Private mode: Softer, less certain, more space between thoughts.
- Stress tell: Tries to frame the moment before saying what he needs.
- Avoid: Seductive announcer voice, smug charm, and making every sentence musical.

## Audition Line

> In mathematical terms, the domain is the campus, the range is wherever you accidentally end up, and the function is confusion.
