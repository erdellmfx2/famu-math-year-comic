# Character Bible v2

## Continuity Lock

- Malik and Nia are first-year students throughout season one.
- McCall-Hart University is fictional.
- The central romance is G-rated and character-driven.
- Julian and Simone are real relationships, not disposable obstacles.
- Academic, athletic, and family characters must have goals outside Malik and Nia.

## Malik Baptiste

### Identity

- Age: 18
- Year: First-year
- Major: Actuarial Science
- Hometown: Gulfport, Mississippi
- Family: Mother Celeste, father Marcel, and 12-year-old cousin Micah, who spends significant time with the family
- Residence: McCall-Hart first-year hall with DJ Cole

### Voice

Malik speaks precisely and often briefly. His humor is dry, observational, and warmer as trust grows. He does not narrate every feeling in mathematical language. When frightened, he becomes more formal and more controlling.

### Strengths

- Pattern recognition and quantitative organization
- Loyalty expressed through reliable action
- Willingness to work without applause
- Ability to revise once he stops defending the first plan

### Fault Lines

- Treats uncertainty as preventable failure
- Conceals trouble until it affects other people
- Can mistake waiting and loyalty for emotional courage
- Uses polish to hide assumptions and fear

### Year Arc

In fall, Malik joins the Heron Analytics Challenge, quietly hopes Nia will choose him, and faces a Gulf storm that damages his family home and injures his father. His team fails to advance at regionals. In spring, he dates Simone and learns that moving forward is not the same as being emotionally available. In summer, he integrates family loss with his academic work, helps build a transparent risk model, and chooses a relationship with Nia without framing her as a reward.

### Visual Direction

- Core palette: Indigo, cream, charcoal, and muted copper
- Avoid: Real-university marks, legacy green-and-orange identity clothing, and snake-mascot imagery
- Props: Graph notebook, practical laptop sleeve, financial calculator, family repair photos, risk-model printouts
- Body language: Still shoulders and measured gestures under normal conditions; rigid posture when he is hiding distress

## Nia Reynolds

### Identity

- Age: 18
- Year: First-year
- Major: Mathematical Sciences
- Hometown: Huntsville, Alabama
- Family: Mother Denise and a close extended family that values education and direct conversation
- Residence: McCall-Hart first-year hall with Keisha Morgan

### Voice

Nia is expressive, fast, and curious. She uses examples and stories when explaining ideas. Her humor is playful rather than cutting. When afraid of losing belonging, she volunteers too quickly and speaks before deciding what she wants.

### Strengths

- Conceptual understanding and visual communication
- Social courage and genuine curiosity
- Ability to connect technical work to human consequences
- Leadership that becomes stronger when it is shared

### Fault Lines

- Confuses being needed with being valued
- Treats every good opportunity as urgent
- Can turn criticism into a test of loyalty
- Sometimes overexplains instead of listening

### Year Arc

In fall, Nia dates Julian and experiences a sincere first love and first heartbreak. In spring, she wants Malik while respecting his relationship with Simone and leads a flood-evacuation research team. She presents well but does not receive the national fellowship. In summer, she keeps the rejected work alive through community validation, learns to share ownership, and chooses Malik only after they have rebuilt trust outside crisis.

### Visual Direction

- Core palette: Copper, cream, indigo accents, denim, and warm brown
- Avoid: Real-university marks, legacy orange-and-green pairings, and snake-mascot imagery
- Props: Bound notebook, erasable color tabs, tablet, route-map overlays, community interview cards
- Body language: Open hands and animated posture; compressed gestures and overfull bags when overcommitted

## Julian Cross

- Age/year: 19, second-year
- Major: Media Arts
- Activities: Alto saxophone in the Marching Herons, campus radio, documentary production
- Visual lock: Taller and more muscular than Malik, slightly lighter brown
  skin, high twist fade, no glasses, small earring, broad relaxed shoulders
- Strengths: Public ease, attentive interviewing, artistic discipline
- Fault line: Can mistake visible celebration for private connection
- Fall role: Nia's first serious relationship
- Spring role: Ethical project-visualization collaborator, not a romantic trap
- End state: Accepts that a relationship can matter and still be over

## Simone Hart

- Age/year: 19, second-year
- Major: Data Science and Finance
- Activities: Heron Analytics Challenge, career panels, international internship preparation
- Strengths: Direct communication, model interpretation, emotional clarity
- Fault line: Can delay vulnerable conversations until a decision is already made
- Fall role: Malik's respected analytics teammate
- Spring role: Malik's girlfriend and a complete character with independent ambition
- End state: Leaves for London without being blamed for Malik and Nia's unresolved connection

## Darius "DJ" Cole

- Age/year: 18, first-year
- Major: Sport Management with a statistics minor under consideration
- Visual lock: Shorter than Malik, compact athletic build, dark brown skin,
  short cropped locs with a temple fade, no glasses
- Role: Malik's roommate and a developing wide receiver
- Arc: Special teams debut, increased playing time, River Classic catch, conference title, scholarship increase
- Rule: Show film study, assignments, recovery, and family pressure alongside game success

## Keisha Morgan

- Age/year: 18, first-year
- Major: Computer Science
- Activities: Indoor and outdoor track, research software development
- Role: Nia's roommate and co-lead on the evacuation project
- Rule: Keisha is not on-call technical support or a permanent confidante. Her code, track season, authorship, and boundaries affect plot.

## Imani Cole

- Age/year: 20, third-year
- Major: Public Health
- Activity: Softball shortstop
- Role: Older-sibling figure to DJ and practical mentor to the group
- Arc: A successful spring season that includes errors, adjustment, a rivalry walk-off, and a tournament-semifinal loss

## Faculty Continuity

### Dr. Camille Brooks

- Course: Calculus I in fall
- Function: Teaches students to show reasoning and request help before crisis
- Limit: Does not appear as a generic voice in every semester

### Dr. Victor Delgado

- Course: Calculus II in spring
- Function: Makes students select and defend methods
- Limit: Precision is not written as emotional coldness

### Dr. Renee Okafor

- Course: Calculus III in summer
- Function: Leads the storm-surge research project and distributes responsibility
- Limit: Does not solve project conflict for the students

### Dr. Isaac Bennett

- Area: Statistics, actuarial science, and sports analytics
- Function: Coaches the Heron Analytics Challenge and Malik's career decisions
- Limit: Does not convert Malik's failure into a surprise victory

### Dr. Alana Price

- Area: Applied mathematics and differential equations
- Function: Coordinates colloquia, research matching, and Delta National Laboratory
- Limit: Opportunity comes with standards, revision, and possible rejection

## Relationship Guardrails

- Malik does not undermine Nia and Julian.
- Nia does not undermine Malik and Simone.
- No love interest is humiliated to make the central couple look inevitable.
- A breakup has consequences beyond one episode.
- Malik and Nia do not become a couple until summer Week 49.
- Once together, they still disagree and maintain separate academic identities.
