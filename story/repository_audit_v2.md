# Repository and Story Audit v2

Audit date: 2026-07-22

## Scope

This audit covers every file in the repository. Text files were validated for UTF-8 readability and structured files were parsed. Images, PDFs, and Word files were checked for basic integrity. Story content was then reviewed by source group, with duplicate generated snapshots treated as copies of their active source files.

## Repository Inventory

- 375 files outside `.git`
- 244 readable text files
- 123 valid PNG/JPEG images
- 6 readable PDFs
- 2 valid DOCX files
- 54 weekly timeline files totaling about 20,333 words
- 54 weekly prose files totaling about 131,958 words
- 54 generated prose snapshots that duplicate the active weekly prose sources

No corrupt files were found.

## Principal Findings

### 1. The generator creates repetition by design

The old master-calendar builder rotates a very small prompt bank across the entire year:

- Four recurring in-semester activities for Malik
- Four recurring in-semester activities for Nia
- Five professor slogans
- One generic `campus slice-of-life` scene hint on 330 of 374 days

That design produced 70 or more repetitions of individual activities such as timed drills, office hours, concept maps, study circles, planning meetings, and career-checklist updates. The prose expanded those repeated prompts instead of introducing new causes, choices, and consequences.

### 2. The prose lacks durable plot pressure

The manuscript frequently names pressure, growth, structure, leadership, discipline, reset, and rhythm, but those ideas rarely attach to a sustained external problem. Weeks often open by referring to the previous week's unanswered question and close by promising another question, invitation, or challenge.

The current prose contains:

- No breakup or heartbreak storyline
- No sustained romantic relationship
- No named family emergency with continuing consequences
- No semester-long competitive objective that ends in a meaningful failure
- No spring reversal of the central emotional dynamics
- No summer romantic resolution

Family scenes are usually generic rest or holiday scenes. Athletic events are often listed as ambient campus energy rather than affecting a character's choices. Research is discussed as possibility for many weeks before a concrete project appears.

### 3. Course sources are richer than the story

The saved syllabi provide strong and usable academic progression:

- Calculus I: functions, limits, continuity, derivatives, derivative applications, antiderivatives, definite integrals, and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
- Calculus II: applications of integration, integration techniques, improper integrals, sequences and series, convergence tests, Taylor and power series, parametric curves, and polar coordinates
- Calculus III: vectors, three-dimensional geometry, vector-valued functions, partial derivatives, gradients, optimization, multiple integrals, coordinate changes, vector fields, and the major integral theorems

The old manuscript names concrete mathematical topics only occasionally. It also gives all five professors interchangeable daily touchpoints, including periods when they would not be teaching the characters.

### 4. Events and extracurricular research are underused

The repository includes football, men's basketball, baseball, and softball dates, plus source material for math clubs, colloquia, peer coaching, conferences, tutoring, and undergraduate research. The old story does not build durable subplots from these resources. It also omits women's basketball, track and field, band culture, laboratory visits, research presentations, and most off-campus academic travel.

### 5. Active files are institution-specific

There are 1,989 matching lines across active text sources containing FAMU names, venues, colors, symbols, or closely tied language. Existing visual assets visibly contain FAMU marks, orange-and-green clothing, the Rattler identity, named buildings, and campus signage.

The visual profiles also conflict with the written character bible by labeling Malik and Nia as sophomores while the story describes them as first-year students.

## Required Correction

The v2 story package must:

1. Use a wholly fictional Deep South HBCU and original visual identity.
2. Treat the old FAMU calendars, syllabi, sports schedules, and campus research only as structural reference material.
3. Give every week a distinct plot function, a decision, a consequence, and a handoff to the next week.
4. Track fall, spring, and summer arcs explicitly rather than relying on recurring motivational language.
5. Tie course topics, athletics, family events, relationships, competitions, talks, conferences, and lab travel to character choices.
6. Keep all comic image production paused until the v2 script receives explicit approval.

## Disposition of Existing Material

- Old prose and generated exports are legacy v1 manuscripts, not active v2 scripts.
- Existing FAMU storyboards, prompts, and generated art are legacy visual studies, not v2 continuity references.
- Saved research sources remain useful as historical planning references and should retain their original source names for provenance.
- Character names and the strongest personality contrast may carry forward, but their arcs, supporting cast, wardrobes, and institutional context require revision.
