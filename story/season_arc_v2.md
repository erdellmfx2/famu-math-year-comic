# Season Arc v2: The Formula of Becoming

## Series Premise

First-year mathematics students Malik Baptiste and Nia Reynolds arrive at fictional McCall-Hart University expecting difficult classes. They do not expect a Gulf storm, first love, jealousy, public failure, family strain, athletic triumphs, or a summer research problem that will force them to stop hiding behind timing. Across one year, they learn that becoming reliable is not the same as controlling every outcome, and that love is not a prize for waiting patiently.

## Story Promise

Each week must advance at least two of these six tracks:

1. Malik and Nia's changing relationship
2. A semester-specific competitive challenge
3. Family pressure and recovery
4. A concrete Calculus I, II, or III progression
5. Athletics or HBCU campus life with consequences
6. Academic life beyond class, including talks, service, conferences, research, and Delta National Laboratory

No week may exist only to repeat study habits or motivational advice.

## Central Characters

### Malik Baptiste

An actuarial science student from Gulfport, Mississippi. Malik is observant, controlled, and funny when he trusts the room. He wants financial stability for his family and mistakes preparedness for protection. A fall storm damages his family home and injures his father during cleanup, forcing him to face problems no spreadsheet can contain.

### Nia Reynolds

A mathematical sciences student from Huntsville, Alabama. Nia is curious, socially brave, and gifted at explanation. She mistakes usefulness for belonging and can make every opportunity feel urgent. Her year teaches her to want things openly, accept a fair loss, and stop turning care into endless availability.

## Supporting Cast

### Julian Cross

A second-year media arts student and alto saxophonist in McCall-Hart's marching band. Julian is attentive, ambitious, and comfortable in public. He and Nia date through the fall. Their relationship is real and affectionate, but homecoming demands, Nia's overcommitment, and different ideas about intimacy expose a mismatch. He remains a full person after the breakup and later helps visualize Nia's research without becoming her romantic reward.

### Simone Hart

A second-year data science and finance student with no family connection to the university founders. Simone is direct, funny, and skilled at translating models into decisions. She and Malik work together on the Heron Analytics Challenge in fall and date in spring. She is never written as a villain or obstacle. In April she ends the relationship because their futures and emotional attention no longer align.

### Darius "DJ" Cole

Malik's roommate and a first-year wide receiver. DJ earns playing time slowly, makes a decisive River Classic catch, and receives a scholarship increase. His sports-success arc includes film study, classes, injury prevention, and pressure from home.

### Keisha Morgan

Nia's roommate, a computer science student and sprinter. Keisha supports Nia without serving only as confidante. She contributes code to the spring evacuation-model project and has her own indoor-to-outdoor track progression.

### Imani Cole

DJ's older sister and McCall-Hart's softball shortstop. Her spring season gives the story a sustained women's athletics thread. She becomes a practical mentor on performance, failure, and returning to the field after mistakes.

## Faculty Roles

- Dr. Camille Brooks teaches fall Calculus I and challenges students to make their reasoning visible.
- Dr. Victor Delgado teaches spring Calculus II and emphasizes selection of methods, not ritual repetition.
- Dr. Renee Okafor teaches summer Calculus III and leads the storm-surge modeling project.
- Dr. Isaac Bennett advises actuarial science and coaches the Heron Analytics Challenge.
- Dr. Alana Price runs applied mathematics seminars and coordinates Delta National Laboratory.

Faculty may guide, question, and open doors. They do not solve the students' personal conflicts.

## Fall Arc: Nia Is Chosen, Malik Waits, Malik Fails

### Romance

Nia and Julian meet during Summer Bridge and begin dating after the Heritage Kickoff. Malik realizes he wants more than friendship with Nia but respects her relationship and does not sabotage it. His private hope contributes to emotional distance, but the story does not reward passive waiting.

### Competitive challenge

Malik joins Simone and other students on the Heron Analytics Challenge, building a fourth-down decision model from football data. The team improves and reaches the regional semifinal, but Malik overfits the model and resists uncertainty. They fail to advance in December. The loss remains a loss.

### Family emergency and loss

A late-September Gulf storm floods part of Malik's family home. His father is injured during cleanup and cannot work for several weeks. Malik misses a quiz, takes a campus job, and conceals the full financial strain until it harms his coursework and friendships. Insurance delays and repairs continue into summer.

### Heartbreak and sports success

Nia and Julian's relationship ends in November after affection proves insufficient to solve incompatible needs. Nia experiences a genuine first heartbreak. During the same period, DJ earns a starting role and makes the River Classic catch that secures a conference-title opportunity. Success and pain share the same campus week.

### Academics

Calculus I moves from limits to derivatives, applications, antiderivatives, definite integrals, and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus. Tests, homework sets, registrar deadlines, and finals have specific dates and consequences.

## Spring Arc: Malik Is Chosen, Nia Waits, Nia Fails

### Reversed romance

Malik and Simone begin dating in January after months of earned trust. Nia is surprised to recognize that she wants Malik just as he stops orbiting her. She respects his relationship, but her hope changes how she interprets study sessions, Valentine's Day, and Malik's growing independence.

### Reversed challenge

Nia, Keisha, and Julian build a flood-evacuation model and public visualization for the Southeastern Undergraduate Mathematics Symposium. Nia learns research design, presents at a campus forum, travels to a conference, and gives a strong final talk. Her proposal is not selected for the national fellowship. Good work does not guarantee the desired award.

### The twist

Julian's spring collaboration with Nia is professional, not a romantic reunion. Simone recognizes that Malik is emotionally divided, but she ends their relationship for her own reasons as well: she has chosen an international summer internship and does not want either of them to pretend that affection erases incompatible paths. When Nia finally has an opening, Malik refuses to make her his rebound. They choose honesty and distance through finals.

### Athletics and extracurricular life

Women's and men's basketball, Imani's softball season, baseball, and Keisha's track meets affect travel, study coverage, campus celebrations, and research data. The students attend a research conference, guest lectures, a math outreach day, and a Delta National Laboratory preview visit.

### Academics

Calculus II moves through integration applications and techniques, improper integrals, sequences and series, convergence, power and Taylor series, parametric equations, and polar coordinates. The flood project grows naturally from accumulated area, approximation, convergence, and polar representation.

## Summer Arc: Work Becomes Useful, Loss Is Integrated, They Choose Each Other

### Shared project

Malik and Nia enter the McCall-Hart Summer Quantitative Scholars program and take Calculus III. Under Dr. Okafor and Dr. Price, they expand Nia's rejected spring project into a storm-surge and evacuation model. They visit Delta National Laboratory, tour a supercomputing center, attend technical talks, and present progress to scientists.

### Family resolution

The model's subject forces Malik to stop separating family loss from academic ambition. Repairs on the Baptiste home are finally completed in June. Nia and other friends help with the last move-back weekend. The family is not restored to an untouched past; it has adapted.

### Romantic resolution

Malik and Nia rebuild trust without using the project as an excuse to avoid direct conversation. They discuss the fall and spring relationships honestly, go on a first date after Summer B begins, and become a couple in July. Their choice is not framed as destiny or a reward for academic success.

### Academic and community payoff

Calculus III moves through vectors, vector-valued motion, partial derivatives, gradients, optimization, multiple integrals, coordinate systems, vector fields, line and surface integrals, and the major integral theorems. Their model is used in a Bellwether emergency-preparedness exercise and shared at the Summer Scholars Showcase. Nia does not retroactively win the fellowship; her rejected work becomes valuable in a different way. Malik is invited to reapply to the analytics challenge next year, but the season does not promise victory.

### Final image

At the Founders' Bell in August, Malik and Nia look toward their second year. They are together, their families and ambitions remain real, and the future is open rather than solved.

## Writing Rules

- Every scene must contain a want, obstacle, choice, and changed condition.
- Do not use professor slogans as interchangeable narration.
- Do not close consecutive weeks with the same abstract question or invitation.
- Let jokes emerge from character, situation, and HBCU campus life.
- Use mathematics as actual work and perception, not a metaphor pasted onto every emotion.
- Preserve G-rated romance while allowing jealousy, disappointment, grief, and honest conflict.
- Do not make Julian or Simone villains.
- Do not let Malik's waiting create an entitlement to Nia.
- Do not erase Nia's symposium loss with a surprise award.
- Do not generate comic art until `story/approval_status.json` records explicit script approval.
