## Event Anchors

- Athletic, cultural, family, or relationship event

## Daily Episode Plan

### Episode NNN: Day, YYYY-MM-DD

Calendar: Include a fixed event only when it occurs that day.

Write one concrete beat containing:

- A viewpoint character who wants something now
- An obstacle that is not merely "pressure"
- A choice the character makes
- A changed condition or consequence

Repeat for each day in the week. Do not assign a recurring Monday-through-Sunday theme.

## Continuity Handoff

Name the unresolved consequence that the next week must pick up. Avoid abstract closers such as "a new challenge waits" or "the future opens wider."

## Production Gate

This is a story-approval script. Do not create new storyboards, prompts, panels, or comic pages until `story/approval_status.json` records explicit script approval.

## Revision Checklist

- Does the week advance at least two long-running tracks?
- Is the mathematics specific and correctly placed in the course sequence?
- Does every sports or campus event affect a choice, schedule, relationship, or consequence?
- Do supporting characters act for reasons beyond helping Malik and Nia?
- Does the week avoid interchangeable professor slogans?
- Does the ending create a concrete handoff rather than an abstract teaser?
- Is the fictional McCall-Hart identity consistent?
- Are FAMU names, marks, colors, venues, and opponents absent from the active script?
