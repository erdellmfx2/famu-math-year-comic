# Series End Cards and Page Mark

## Approved Assets

The files in `approved/` are the approved v1 visual system for *The Formula of
Becoming*.

- `formula-of-becoming-series-logo-v1.png`: Series title logo.
- `thank-you-end-card-v1.png`: Closing gratitude card.
- `mathematics-invitation-end-card-v1.png`: Mathematics-interest invitation.
- `famu-mathematics-attribution-end-card-v1.png`: Plain-text creator attribution.
- `formula-of-becoming-famu-math-page-mark-v1.png`: Transparent compact page
  mark for the bottom-right corner of comic pages.

## Use Rules

- Place the transparent page mark at the bottom-right of every final comic page.
- Keep a clear margin from page edges and dialogue; it must be visible but not
  compete with the story.
- Use one approved end card as the final page or final panel of each released
  comic sequence. Rotate the three message cards when appropriate.
- The FAMU wording is plain text attribution only. Do not add, imitate, or
  imply an official FAMU logo, seal, mascot, building, or color identity.
- Do not use the review copies as final production assets.
