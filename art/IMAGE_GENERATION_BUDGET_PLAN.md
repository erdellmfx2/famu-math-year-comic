# V2 Image Production Budget Plan

## Current Hold

Do not generate images while `story/approval_status.json` marks the script or comic production unapproved. This plan applies only after the gate opens.

## Principle

Build reusable continuity before episode pages. A smaller, coherent asset library is more valuable than many one-off images.

## Post-Approval Priority

1. Character turnarounds for Malik, Nia, Julian, Simone, DJ, Keisha, and Imani
2. Expression and wardrobe sheets tied to actual v2 scenes
3. Recurring McCall-Hart locations
4. Duo and research-team compositions
5. Episode-specific pages

## Conservative Working Budget

- Asset-building week: 8 to 14 new images
- Episode-production week: 6 to 12 page or repair generations
- Revision week: 2 to 6 replacement generations
- Stop after three failed attempts at the same deliverable and review the source prompt or continuity inputs

These are planning ceilings, not targets. Generate only assets attached to an approved storyboard or continuity need.

## Two-Pass Page Workflow

1. Generate one complete four-panel page without text.
2. Review layout, continuity, and negative space.
3. Edit the same page to add exact approved lettering.
4. Review spelling, balloon order, and mobile readability.

## Cost and Continuity Rules

- Check the active asset library before generating a new pose or location.
- Do not use archived legacy art as a reference.
- Save every approved v2 asset under the correct active week, episode, or asset folder.
- Do not generate speculative pages ahead of script approval.
- Do not trade character or setting continuity for higher volume.
