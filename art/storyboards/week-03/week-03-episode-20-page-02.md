## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Outside Porter
Wide establishing panel outside the Porter Center. Simone leans against a limestone-and-wrought-iron railing as Malik exits through glass doors.

**Simone:** `How bad?`
**Malik:** `One probability problem.`

### Panel 2: Not My Question
Simone turns toward him with direct patience; Malik remains oriented toward the building.

**Simone:** `That was not my question.`
**Malik:** `I may have treated dependent events as independent, corrected it, then failed to justify the correction.`

### Panel 3: Worst Available Conclusion
Simone studies Malik while he looks back through the glass as though the test were still visible.

**Simone:** `You are doing that thing.`
**Malik:** `What thing?`
**Simone:** `Turning incomplete information into the worst available conclusion, then calling it realism.`

### Panel 4: Accurate
They stand side by side at the railing with space between them, the conversation respected rather than romanticized.

**Simone:** `Results come next week. Until then, you do not know.`
**Malik:** `Uncertainty is not comforting.`
**Simone:** `It is not supposed to be. It is supposed to be accurate.`
