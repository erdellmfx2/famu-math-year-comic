## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Two Arrivals
Wide establishing panel of the shared residence room. Keisha's side is minimal and scheduled; Nia's side has four bags, a leafy plant, and a stack of organization flyers.

**Caption:** `Across campus, Nia's new roommate was saving her from herself.`

### Panel 2: The Count
Keisha studies the stack of flyers while Nia counts on her fingers.

**Keisha:** `How many of those did you join?`
**Nia:** `Join is a strong word.`

### Panel 3: Six
Nia finishes counting with a sheepish smile. Keisha extends one hand, calm and decisive.

**Nia:** `Six.`
**Keisha:** `Phone.`

### Panel 4: Intervention
Nia clutches the phone lightly while Keisha remains composed beside her precise wall calendar and carefully packed computer.

**Nia:** `Why?`
**Keisha:** `Because this is an intervention.`
