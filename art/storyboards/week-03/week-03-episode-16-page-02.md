## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: The List
Both roommates sit on the floor sorting six flyers into keep, maybe, and later piles.

**Keisha:** `You also have Bridge, classes starting soon, and whatever is happening with Saxophone Man.`
**Nia:** `His name is Julian.`

### Panel 2: What Could Matter
Nia moves a flyer into the maybe pile, her confidence giving way to worry.

**Nia:** `I just do not want to miss the thing that could matter.`

### Panel 3: Not All of It Is Yours
Keisha sits on the edge of her bed and speaks with calm directness. Nia listens, still.

**Keisha:** `Everything matters to somebody. That does not make all of it yours.`
**Caption:** `The sentence quieted the room.`

### Panel 4: First Room
Keisha returns the phone after Nia reduces the list. Nia looks relieved but not fully convinced.

**Keisha:** `You did not close doors. You chose which room to stand in first.`
**Nia:** `Six clubs counts as an emergency?`
**Keisha:** `Six clubs before classes absolutely counts.`
