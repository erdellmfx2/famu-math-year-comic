## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Convocation Morning
Wide establishing panel at the end of Cypress Walk under a washed-denim sky. First-year students gather in dress clothes and fresh fictional MHU colors beneath indigo banners.

**Caption:** `Opening Convocation began under a sky the color of washed denim.`

### Panel 2: Four First-Years
Malik stands with DJ near the live oaks as Nia arrives with Keisha. The four merge into the larger crowd.

**Caption:** `The four first-years joined the crowd.`

### Panel 3: Before Breakfast
DJ leans toward Malik; Keisha and Nia exchange amused looks.

**DJ:** `This is a lot of symbolism before breakfast.`
**Malik:** `It is ten thirty.`
**Keisha:** `Your people are strange.`
**Nia:** `They are growing on me.`

### Panel 4: The Bell
The Founders' Bell rings beneath its limestone arch. Conversations stop, phones lower, and the four friends share the silence.

**Caption:** `For a few seconds, thousands of individual worries shared one silence.`
