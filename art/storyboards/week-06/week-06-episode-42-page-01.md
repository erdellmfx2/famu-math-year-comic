## Panel Plan
### Panel 1: Fair
Nia approaches Julian outside the media building with her planner in hand.

**Nia:** `I was unfair Sunday.`
**Julian:** `A little.`

### Panel 2: The Choice
Nia identifies the choice and Julian grants the small shared responsibility without absorbing it.

**Nia:** `I canceled my study block anyway, then acted like you made the choice.`
**Julian:** `I could have been clearer about how busy it would be.`

### Panel 3: Clear
Nia points out that he said no pressure; Julian smiles but lets her continue.

**Nia:** `You said no pressure. That is clear in at least two languages.`

### Panel 4: Protected Study
Nia states the boundary in a calm, direct way.

**Nia:** `I like showing up for you. But I cannot organize my whole schedule around surprise rehearsal time. I have a protected study block tonight. If you get free, I am still studying.`
