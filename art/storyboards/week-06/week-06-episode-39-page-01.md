## Panel Plan
### Panel 1: Decorative Unconsciousness
Keisha finds Nia asleep over her open calculus book in the morning study lounge.

**Keisha:** `This is not studying. This is decorative unconsciousness.`

### Panel 2: Why?
Keisha asks why Nia went to rehearsal; Nia answers simply.

**Keisha:** `Were you performing?`
**Nia:** `No.`
**Keisha:** `Then why?`

### Panel 3: The Charge
Nia explains the invitation and short break while Keisha packs her track bag.

**Keisha:** `You volunteered your study time, then charged Julian emotionally when the thing he told you would happen happened.`

### Panel 4: Soft or Useful
Nia asks whether Keisha is being harsh; Keisha offers useful honesty.

**Nia:** `That sounds harsh.`
**Keisha:** `Do you want soft or useful? I can schedule soft tonight.`
**Nia:** `Useful.`
