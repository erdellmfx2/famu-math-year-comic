**Purpose:** Julian names Nia's unasked sacrifice gently, leaving her to recognize that her resentment was unfair.

## Panel Plan
### Panel 1: Moved Block
Outside the stadium, Julian asks about the study block and Nia says she moved it.

**Julian:** `I thought you had a study block.`
**Nia:** `I moved it.`

### Panel 2: Tone
Julian asks why she sounds angry; Nia avoids admitting the feeling.

**Julian:** `Then why do you sound mad at me?`
**Nia:** `I do not.`

### Panel 3: No Pressure
Julian reminds her that he said no pressure, neither defensive nor cold.

**Julian:** `I also said no pressure.`

### Panel 4: Not Asked
Nia begins to leave. Julian lets her go rather than chasing the argument.

**Julian:** `I was glad you came. But I did not ask you to give up your afternoon.`
**Caption:** `He was right.`
