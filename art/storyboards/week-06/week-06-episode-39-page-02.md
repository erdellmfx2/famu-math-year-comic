## Panel Plan
### Panel 1: Availability
Keisha speaks directly across Nia's crowded planner.

**Keisha:** `You keep treating availability like proof of affection.`

### Panel 2: Care
Nia insists that she cares; Keisha agrees, then names the distinction.

**Nia:** `I do care.`
**Keisha:** `I know. But care is not the same as having no boundaries.`

### Panel 3: Access
Nia asks what happens if she says no. Keisha's answer lands without drama.

**Nia:** `What if I say no too often and people stop asking?`
**Keisha:** `Then they wanted access, not you.`

### Panel 4: Indigo
Nia marks Thursday's review session as protected time in indigo; Keisha approves.

**Keisha:** `Restore it. Tell Julian. Do not make it dramatic.`
**Nia:** `Protected time.`
**Keisha:** `Good. Now wash the equation off your face.`
