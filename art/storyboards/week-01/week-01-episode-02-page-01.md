## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: The Same Table

Wide setting introduction. Morning light enters the Quantitative Futures Lab. Malik has already claimed the table by the outlet; orderly readiness problems, calculator, and laptop form a quiet perimeter. Nia arrives with iced coffee and her tabbed notebook, taking the chair across from him. Leave upper-left space for a caption and center space for balloons.

**Caption:** `Saturday's Bridge session was optional, which meant Nia arrived ten minutes late carrying iced coffee and insisting that optional mornings should begin at noon.`

**Nia:** `Look at us. A tradition.`

**Malik:** `It has happened twice.`

### Panel 2: The Hidden Sheet

Medium close two-shot at the table. Malik reflexively lowers his laptop screen as another student passes, his posture rigid. Nia notices but does not lean in. Keep their hands and the laptop edge visible; leave lower-right space for dialogue.

**Nia:** `You do that every time somebody walks by.`

**Malik:** `It is private.`

**Nia:** `Private is fine. Panicked is different.`

### Panel 3: Earned Access

Tighter shot. Nia leans back, palms open in a quick apology; Malik's shoulders release slightly as he reopens the budget. His face carries guarded fear, not melodrama. Leave negative space above the two figures.

**Nia:** `Sorry. That came out like I had earned access. I haven't.`

**Malik:** `My family budget was balanced when I left. Now it isn't. I told them it was.`

**Nia:** `Was that a lie?`

### Panel 4: Two Boxes

Overhead-to-medium collaborative shot. Nia draws two large boxes in her bound notebook, labeled only with simple abstract lines in the unlettered pass; Malik studies them, then lets out an unexpected laugh. Keep the notebook broad enough for the later balloons but do not render readable notebook text in pass one.

**Nia:** `Then it was a model with new information.`

**Nia:** `It is me stopping you from staring at one red number like it has been elected president of your life.`

**Caption:** `Together they found a duplicate textbook estimate and a subscription Malik could pause. The balance returned to black.`
