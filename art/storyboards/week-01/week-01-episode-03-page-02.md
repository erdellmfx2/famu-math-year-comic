# Week 01, Episode 03, Page 02: Draw It First - Meaning Before Variables

**Story sources:** `story/timeline-weeks/1.md`; `story/timeline-weeks-prose-v2/prose_1.md`  
**Purpose:** Let the modeling disagreement become an actual exchange and show their first negotiated method.

## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Forty Minutes
Two-shot at the diagnostic task. Malik points to a timer and Nia turns the page sideways to sketch families near a bus stop.

**Malik:** `We have forty minutes.`  
**Nia:** `I know.`  
**Malik:** `Then why are there people?`  
**Nia:** `Because people buy the boxes.`

### Panel 2: Variables Need Meaning
Split-focus close-up of Malik's structured formula and Nia's visual situation map.

**Malik:** `The model needs variables.`  
**Nia:** `And the variables need meaning.`  
**Nia:** `If the pickup site is hard to reach, lowering the price might not increase subscribers the way we expect.`

### Panel 3: What the Data Does Not Say
Malik points at the small data table; Nia writes a limitations note beside the sketch.

**Malik:** `None of that is in the data.`  
**Nia:** `Then we write that it is not in the data.`

### Panel 4: First Model
They rotate laptop and notebook toward each other across the table, finally collaborating.

**Malik:** `Suppose we label my formula a first model and list your factors as limitations.`  
**Nia:** `Suppose we also explain what information we would collect next.`  
**Malik:** `Fine.`
