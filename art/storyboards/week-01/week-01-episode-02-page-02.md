# Week 01, Episode 02, Page 02: Room at the Table - New Information

**Story sources:** `story/timeline-weeks/1.md`; `story/timeline-weeks-prose-v2/prose_1.md`
**Purpose:** Expand Nia and Malik's first real conversation about privacy, panic, and a changing model.

## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: A Tradition
Morning two-shot at their familiar table. Nia arrives with iced coffee; Malik has already opened readiness problems.

**Nia:** `Look at us. A tradition.`
**Malik:** `It has happened twice.`
**Nia:** `Every tradition starts before somebody puts it on a T-shirt.`

### Panel 2: Private and Panicked
Nia notices Malik minimize the budget as another student passes. Malik's shoulders tighten, then Nia leans back to give him space.

**Nia:** `Private is fine. Panicked is different.`
**Nia:** `Sorry. That came out like I had earned access. I haven't.`

### Panel 3: A Model With New Information
Malik reopens the file. Nia listens without touching the laptop, her expression serious.

**Malik:** `My family budget was balanced when I left. Now it isn't. I told them it was.`
**Nia:** `Was that a lie?`
**Malik:** `No.`
**Nia:** `Then it was a model with new information.`

### Panel 4: Two Boxes
Overhead view of Nia's notebook: two hand-drawn boxes in pass one, no readable writing. Malik laughs while they circle a duplicate textbook estimate and a paused subscription.

**Nia:** `It is me stopping you from staring at one red number like it has been elected president of your life.`

## Art Rules
- Finished four-panel phone-readable page; no text in pass one.
- Keep the tone kind, with Malik's relieved laughter earned rather than exaggerated.
