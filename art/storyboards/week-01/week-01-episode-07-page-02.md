# Week 01, Episode 07, Page 02: Accountability - What Was Not Said

**Story sources:** `story/timeline-weeks/1.md`; `story/timeline-weeks-prose-v2/prose_1.md`  
**Purpose:** Expand the post-presentation banter and allow the Julian handoff to land without rushing.

## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: After Arguing
Copper-sunset two-shot beneath Founders' Bell. Malik and Nia carry their laptops after the presentation, relaxed and pleased.

**Nia:** `We work well together.`  
**Malik:** `After arguing.`  
**Nia:** `During arguing. The arguing is part of the system.`

### Panel 2: Someone Delightful
Malik leans lightly against the limestone arch; Nia holds his gaze with a grin.

**Malik:** `That sounds unhealthy.`  
**Nia:** `Only because you hate being challenged by someone delightful.`

### Panel 3: If You Have to Ask
They begin walking from the bell as the distant drumline becomes a warm visual rhythm.

**Nia:** `If you have to ask, you are already in one.`

### Panel 4: Friday's Showcase
Nia looks briefly vulnerable as she mentions Julian; Malik adjusts his laptop-bag strap before answering with practiced calm.

**Nia:** `I think he might actually be asking me out.`  
**Malik:** `You should go.`  
**Caption:** `They walked away from the bell together, carrying two kinds of plans into the next week.`

## Art Rules
- Finished four-panel phone-readable page; no text in pass one.
