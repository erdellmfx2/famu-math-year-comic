# Week 01, Episode 01, Page 02: The Red Number - After the Joke

**Story sources:** `story/timeline-weeks/1.md`; `story/timeline-weeks-prose-v2/prose_1.md`  
**Purpose:** Give Malik and Nia's first exchange room to establish warmth, boundaries, and Malik's private pressure.

## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Lunch Check-In
Medium two-shot at the Summer Bridge lunch table. Nia notices Malik correcting the family budget again.

**Nia:** `You fixed the accusation?`  
**Malik:** `I moved the expense, reduced two categories, and added a warning rule.`

### Panel 2: Corporate Yelling
Closer on Nia, amused but gentle. Malik's screen shows a simple green balance indicator with no readable numbers.

**Nia:** `So your spreadsheet can yell before the problem gets bad.`  
**Malik:** `It does not yell. It changes color.`  
**Nia:** `Corporate yelling.`

### Panel 3: The Unshown Text
Quiet close-up of Malik holding his phone face-down beside the repaired budget; Nia does not push.

**Caption:** `He showed her the repaired balance. He did not show her the text from his mother asking whether everything still worked.`

### Panel 4: An Answer That Stays
Wide exit shot. Nia tilts her head toward the laptop, open rather than demanding; Malik looks after her, considering.

**Nia:** `Do you need help?`  
**Malik:** `I can fix it.`  
**Nia:** `I didn't ask if you could.`

- Finished four-panel phone-readable page; no text in pass one.
