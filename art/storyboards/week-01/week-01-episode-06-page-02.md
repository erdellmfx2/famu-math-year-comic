# Week 01, Episode 06, Page 02: Indigo Night - Two Kinds of News

**Story sources:** `story/timeline-weeks/1.md`; `story/timeline-weeks-prose-v2/prose_1.md`
**Purpose:** Give the night's humor, Julian's invitation, and Malik's unspoken reaction room to land.

## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Not the Whole University
At a festival table, Nia reaches for another interest-card pen and Malik gently takes it away, amused but concerned.

**Malik:** `You cannot join the whole university tonight.`
**Nia:** `Watch me.`
**Malik:** `I am watching. It is concerning.`

### Panel 2: The Invitation
Near the campus radio booth after the saxophone set, Julian speaks to Nia with relaxed confidence; Malik is not in this panel.

**Julian:** `Occasional rescuer of boring events.`
**Nia:** `This event was not boring.`
**Julian:** `Then I can take the night off.`

### Panel 3: Choosing Things
At Copper Cup Cafe, Nia and Malik reunite with their interest materials. The festival glows behind them.

**Nia:** `I got invited to the radio showcase.`
**Malik:** `I signed up for an analytics information session.`
**Nia:** `Look at us choosing things.`

### Panel 4: Seven, Technically
Julian gives Nia a distant two-finger salute. She smiles; Malik's attention catches on the moment before he looks away.

**Malik:** `You chose six things.`
**Nia:** `Seven, technically.`
**Caption:** `Malik did not yet have a name for the small drop inside his chest.`
