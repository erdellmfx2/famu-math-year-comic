# Week 01, Episode 05, Page 02: The Campus Has a Memory - Steps and Leaps

**Story sources:** `story/timeline-weeks/1.md`; `story/timeline-weeks-prose-v2/prose_1.md`
**Purpose:** Expand the tour's banter and give Founders' Bell the emotional room it has in the prose.

## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Map and Camera
Two-shot along Cypress Walk. Nia photographs a campus detail while Malik carefully studies the paper map.

**Nia:** `You know they give us that online.`
**Malik:** `Phones lose signal.`
**Nia:** `Paper maps exist.`
**Malik:** `Paper gets wet.`

### Panel 2: Preparing for Joy
Nia lowers the camera and looks at Malik with honest curiosity; he considers the question.

**Nia:** `Do you prepare for joy like this too?`
**Malik:** `I have not had to.`

### Panel 3: The Bell
Wide upward establishing shot beneath Founders' Bell and its limestone arch. Nia looks up; indigo banners move in humid air.

**Nia:** `Imagine hearing that on your last day here.`
**Malik:** `We have to make it to the first day first.`

### Panel 4: Documentation
They continue beneath the live oaks, Nia laughing at Malik's seriousness.

**Nia:** `You really refuse to skip steps.`
**Malik:** `Steps exist for a reason.`
**Nia:** `So do leaps.`
**Malik:** `Leaps are just steps with poor documentation.`

## Art Rules
- Finished four-panel phone-readable page; no text in pass one.
- Emphasize the map, camera, bell, limestone, live oaks, and civic campus atmosphere.
