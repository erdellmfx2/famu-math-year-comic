## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Safe
Celeste reaches Malik from a relative's phone; Malik stands sharply at his desk.

Malik asks about Marcel; his mother pauses.

**Malik:** `Where is Daddy?`

**Celeste:** `He cut his leg bad and twisted his knee. Mr. Landry took him to urgent care.`

**Celeste:** `We do not even know which roads are open.`
**Malik:** `I will find one.`
