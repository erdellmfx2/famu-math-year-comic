# Week 08, Episode 56, Page 02: The Station - No Model

## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Food Bag
Nia offers Malik food and non-demanding contact.

**Nia:** `You do not have to text updates. But if you want to, I will answer.`

### Panel 2: Waiting
Malik waits under station lights as thoughts grow louder.

**Caption:** `The waiting made every thought louder.`

### Panel 3: Fear
Malik tells Nia what he cannot estimate.

**Malik:** `I do not know what they will come back to.`
**Nia:** `You will not face it by yourself.`

### Panel 4: Board
Malik hugs DJ, then Nia, and boards the bus.

**Caption:** `Then he boarded with no model for the house waiting at the other end.`
