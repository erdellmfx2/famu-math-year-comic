# Week 08, Episode 52, Page 01: Help Without Taking Over - The Table

## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Logistics Center
Nia arrives early and over-organizes a storm-relief table.

**Caption:** `Nia began designing a tracking sheet before remembering that nobody had asked her to run the operation.`

### Panel 2: Coordinator
Imani arrives with boxes and names the situation.

**Nia:** `You must be DJ's sister.`
**Imani:** `And you must be the person who turned my folding table into a logistics center.`

### Panel 3: Ask First
Nia asks whether she did too much; Imani gives useful advice.

**Nia:** `Too much?`
**Imani:** `Useful. But ask before you redesign somebody's whole system.`

### Panel 4: Verified Requests
They sort supplies together.

**Caption:** `Useful relief work depended on verified requests, not on collecting whatever made donors feel generous.`
