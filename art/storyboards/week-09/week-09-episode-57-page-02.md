# Week 09, Episode 57, Page 02: The Inventory - Useful

## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Inventory
Malik builds an abstract item inventory at the dining table while neighbors clean around him.

**Caption:** `Every item resisted becoming a number.`

### Panel 2: Policy
Celeste lifts damp school papers; Malik reaches for procedure.

**Malik:** `Take pictures before we throw anything away.`
**Celeste:** `I know.`

### Panel 3: Stop
Malik starts citing insurance policy; Celeste is exhausted.

**Malik:** `The policy may require--`
**Celeste:** `Malik.`

### Panel 4: Porch
Marcel's leg buckles. Malik helps him toward the porch instead of arguing.

**Marcel:** `Help me to the porch.`
**Caption:** `It was the first task Malik completed all day that felt entirely useful.`
