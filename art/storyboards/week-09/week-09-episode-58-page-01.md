# Week 09, Episode 58, Page 01: Rate of Rising Water - Tank Math

## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Packet
Malik sits at Aunt Renee's kitchen table with an abstract related-rates packet.

**Caption:** `A textbook had invented polite water that entered a perfect container at a steady rate and damaged nothing.`

### Panel 2: Same Water
Micah joins him in borrowed slides and studies the blank diagram.

**Micah:** `Is that flood math?`
**Malik:** `Tank math.`

### Panel 3: Real Question
Micah looks toward the insurance call in the next room.

**Micah:** `How fast did the water come in our house?`

### Panel 4: House Footprint
Malik draws an abstract house footprint and chooses to explain.

**Malik:** `If we estimate how much floor area the water covered, and how the depth changed over time, we can estimate how quickly the amount of water inside was growing.`
