# Week 09, Episode 60, Page 02: Official - Yes

## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Not Casual
Julian tells Nia he wants a real name for what they have.

**Julian:** `I would like to call you my girlfriend. I would like to be your boyfriend.`

### Panel 2: Reason
He makes the request without forcing it.

**Julian:** `Not because a label makes everything different, but because I do not want to act casual about something that is not casual to me.`

### Panel 3: Yes
Nia gives him the answer.

**Nia:** `Yes. I want that too.`

### Panel 4: Second Speech
Julian admits he had prepared for hesitation; Nia laughs and they kiss.

**Julian:** `I had a whole second speech if you asked for time.`
**Nia:** `Was it better?`
**Julian:** `Much worse.`
