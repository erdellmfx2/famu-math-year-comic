# Week 09, Episode 58, Page 02: Rate of Rising Water - Range

## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Game
Micah asks when rising water reached his game console.

**Micah:** `Can we find when it got to my game?`
**Malik:** `Probably within a range. Not an exact minute.`

### Panel 2: Deep Fast
They mark visual-only lines that show the increase after the threshold.

**Micah:** `So it got deep fast.`
**Malik:** `Yes.`

### Panel 3: Could We
Micah asks the question underneath the question.

**Micah:** `Could we have moved more stuff?`

### Panel 4: Replaceable
Malik answers without letting Micah carry blame.

**Malik:** `Maybe a few things. But nobody should have stayed inside trying to beat the water. Things can be replaced. You cannot.`
**Micah:** `Even the game?`
**Malik:** `Even the game.`
