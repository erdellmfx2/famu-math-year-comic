## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Closed Booth

Establishing view of the quiet Media House radio booth exterior and second-floor
hall: closed doors, rain-muted light, no readable signs. Nia and Julian meet
with his saxophone case and shoulder strap.

**Caption:** `Labor Day closed the university, but not everyone had received the memo.`

### Panel 2: Rain-Bright Brick

Nia and Julian walk Cypress Walk under wet live oaks; the honey-brick path
shines after an early shower.

**Caption:** `The date had stretched around practices and responsibilities instead of unfolding in one perfect block.`

### Panel 3: Forty Percent More

Julian looks pleased while Nia gestures with playful seriousness as they walk.

**Julian:** `Did you enjoy the game?`
**Nia:** `I understood at least forty percent more because of you.`

### Panel 4: The Wrist Tape

They pause by the side entrance in softened daylight. Julian makes a confident
joke; Nia gives him a skeptical, happy look.

**Julian:** `About the handsome posture too?`
**Nia:** `The evidence was inconclusive.`
