## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Laundry Mountain

In the residence room, DJ searches a laundry pile for a clean practice shirt
while Malik reads an unreadable phone message at his desk.

**Caption:** `Simone's message arrived while DJ was searching for a clean practice shirt.`

### Panel 2: Trial Seat

Malik reads again, then says the news out loud with controlled disbelief.

**Malik:** `I have a trial seat.`
**DJ:** `On analytics?`

### Panel 3: Two Weeks

DJ drops two shirts and grabs Malik by the shoulders in celebratory motion;
Malik remains dryly overwhelmed.

**Malik:** `Two weeks.`
**DJ:** `My roommate is moving up in the world!`

### Panel 4: Nerdy Yes

DJ asks whether Malik's chart helped; Malik answers with a careful caveat,
while DJ visibly understands the answer anyway.

**DJ:** `Did your chart help us win?`
**Malik:** `It helped identify one second-half adjustment. Football is not a controlled experiment.`
**DJ:** `So yes, but nerdy.`
