## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: No Call

DJ waits on the sideline in fictional Herons game uniform, shifting on his
toes near a coordinator while another player enters the special-teams unit.

**Caption:** `Each time the return unit gathered, another name was called.`

### Panel 2: The Win

From the student section, Malik watches DJ celebrate with teammates after the
win; in the distance Nia finds Julian's copper wrist tape in the band block.

**Caption:** `McCall-Hart won by ten.`

### Panel 3: Dark Room

Back in the first-year residence room, DJ sets his helmet down and sits in
semi-darkness; Malik stands in the doorway, gentle and direct.

**Malik:** `You okay?`
**DJ:** `We won.`

### Panel 4: Ready

DJ looks tired rather than angry; Malik sits across from him, offering no
easy certainty.

**DJ:** `I was ready. Nobody needed me to be.`
**Malik:** `They will.`
**DJ:** `You know that?`
**Malik:** `No.`
