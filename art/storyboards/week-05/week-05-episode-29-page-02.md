## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Submitted

At the table, Nia closes her notebook with relief while Malik checks a final
comma in his explanation. Their phones sit nearby, unreadable.

**Nia:** `We are free.`
**Malik:** `We have reading for Tuesday.`

### Panel 2: The Terrace

Establishing view of the North Star terrace: honey brick, pale stone rail,
students passing in game-day colors, food-truck haze, and rain-dark clouds.

**Nia:** `You are spiritually exhausting.`

### Panel 3: Five Weeks

Nia's phone lies face-down after a pleasant message; she asks about Malik's
analytics work plan. Malik has the almost-smile of someone caught.

**Nia:** `Are you presenting the enormous work plan Simone told you not to build?`
**Malik:** `She did not tell me not to build it.`

### Panel 4: Another Minute

The two sit close but not touching, bags at their feet. Nia has stood to go,
yet both remain seated in the late-afternoon terrace light.

**Nia:** `We should go.`
**Malik:** `Yes.`
**Caption:** `Neither moved for another minute.`
