## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Happy You Are Happy

Malik meets Nia's eyes after the long pause. His gentleness is real, even where the sentence remains incomplete.

**Malik:** `I am happy you are happy. I may also need a little time to adjust.`

### Panel 2: To What?

Nia's concern sharpens. Malik turns toward the campus path rather than answering fully.

**Nia:** `Adjust to what?`

### Panel 3: Schedule

Malik gives the practical true answer about new commitments and accountability check-ins. Keep his face visible enough to show it is not the whole answer.

**Malik:** `Our schedule. You have more commitments. I have the analytics trial.`

### Panel 4: Not Cancel

Nia accepts the plan without accepting a disappearance. They stand beside the bell in late-day copper light, connected but changed.

**Nia:** `We can move them. Not cancel them.`
**Malik:** `Agreed.`
**Caption:** `It was true. It was not the whole truth.`
