## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Near the Stairs

Nia catches Amara beside the limestone stairs outside Eliza Moss Hall.

**Nia:** `I am sorry. I saw your hand and kept talking anyway.`
**Amara:** `You probably did not mean to.`

### Panel 2: Time to Speak

Amara adjusts her bag strap while Nia listens without rushing in.

**Amara:** `Sometimes I take longer to decide how I want to say something. By the time I am ready, the class has moved on.`

### Panel 3: Leave Some Air

Nia's open posture softens into a real commitment.

**Nia:** `Next time, I can leave some air in the room.`
**Amara:** `You do not have to stop answering.`

### Panel 4: Community Service

Later in Nia and Keisha's residence room, Keisha laces running shoes while
Nia sits on the bed with a pillow ready to throw.

**Keisha:** `You apologized? You know what to do differently? Then do that. Guilt is not community service.`
