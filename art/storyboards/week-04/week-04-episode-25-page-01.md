## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: A Second Brain

Fast-moving first-day campus is visible through the residence window. DJ drops
two non-readable play-call printouts on Malik's desk.

**DJ:** `I need a second brain.`
**Malik:** `What happened to the first one?`

### Panel 2: Tables Arguing

DJ points to the two printouts; Malik reads their differing headers.

**DJ:** `Coach wants us to learn tendencies. These tables look like they are arguing.`

### Panel 3: Human Language

Malik marks the key difference with a pencil while DJ leans in.

**Malik:** `Different domains.`
**DJ:** `In human language?`

### Panel 4: Both Questions

Malik makes a small visual comparison chart while explaining; keep charts
unreadable and leave room above their hands.

**Malik:** `The first tells you what happened against everyone. The second tells you what happened against the strongest unit.`
