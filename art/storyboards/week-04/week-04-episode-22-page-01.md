## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: A Graph That Looks Like It Is Lying

Wide establishing view of Eliza Moss Hall's chalkboard seminar room. Dr.
Camille Brooks has drawn a simple graph: a curve approaching an unmarked open
circle at one height and a solid dot directly above it. Keep the graph visible
but use no readable numerals. Malik is poised to answer; Nia watches; Amara is
two rows ahead with her notebook.

**Caption:** `On the first morning of Calculus I, Dr. Brooks drew a graph that looked like it was lying.`

### Panel 2: The First Answer

Medium shot of Dr. Brooks at the board and Malik raising his hand immediately.

**Dr. Brooks:** `What is the answer?`
**Malik:** `Seven. The filled point gives the function value.`

### Panel 3: Correct and Incomplete

Close view of Malik lowering his hand while Dr. Brooks remains calm. Leave
open negative space beside Dr. Brooks, not over either face.

**Dr. Brooks:** `Correct. And incomplete.`

### Panel 4: The Answer Beside It

Three-quarter class view. Dr. Brooks gestures at the open circle; Nia leans
forward with her copper pen; Malik studies the two points.

**Dr. Brooks:** `The function's value is seven. But what value does the graph approach?`
**Nia:** `Four.`
