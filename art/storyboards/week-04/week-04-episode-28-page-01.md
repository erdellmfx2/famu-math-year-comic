## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Copper Earrings

Nia emerges from the residence hall, carefully dressed but trying to appear
effortless. Malik crosses the courtyard and pauses.

**Nia:** `Too much?`
**Malik:** `For an outdoor event in August? Possibly.`

### Panel 2: Properly

Nia clarifies with a small turn; Malik looks at her with restrained sincerity.

**Nia:** `I meant the outfit.`
**Malik:** `You look nice. Very nice.`

### Panel 3: Ready?

Julian approaches from Cypress Walk with a saxophone case and two event passes.

**Julian:** `Ready?`
**Nia:** `Good luck at analytics review.`

### Panel 4: The Window

Nia and Julian walk away together. Malik watches until DJ calls down from the
residence window above him.

**DJ:** `My brother! That is what feelings look like when you refuse to process them!`
**Malik:** `Close the window.`
