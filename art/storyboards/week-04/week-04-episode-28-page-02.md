## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Blank Project Document

At Porter Center, Malik opens a blank project document and rapidly builds an
ambitious but non-readable dashboard plan.

**Caption:** `At Porter Center, he created a dashboard no one had requested.`

### Panel 2: Six Weeks

Simone reads the printed plan over Malik's shoulder, one hand on the chair;
he looks determined rather than rested.

**Simone:** `This is six weeks of work.`
**Malik:** `I can finish a first version in ten days.`

### Panel 3: Why?

Simone asks the question plainly; Malik's answer is technically true but too
small for the feeling beneath it.

**Simone:** `Why?`
**Malik:** `To contribute.`

### Panel 4: Nearby

Simone stands beside him with steady concern; Malik adds another deadline to
the plan, avoiding eye contact.

**Simone:** `You are allowed to have a life outside proving you should be in this room.`
**Caption:** `Officially, he was an alternate. Nearby, he was working like a starter.`
