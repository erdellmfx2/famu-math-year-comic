## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Temporary Seats

Wide review workshop with every desk occupied. Nia stands near her seat,
eagerly gesturing at three simple non-readable graph sketches on the board.

**Caption:** `Saturday's review workshop was crowded with students still deciding whether Calculus I belonged on their schedules.`

### Panel 2: The First Two Graphs

Nia explains rapidly as Dr. Brooks watches for the other raised hands.

**Nia:** `On the first graph, both sides approach three, so the limit is three even though the point is missing.`

### Panel 3: Pause

Nia moves toward a third graph, then freezes as Dr. Brooks gently interrupts.
Amara's hand is visibly halfway raised.

**Nia:** `On the second, the left side approaches negative one and the right side approaches two, so the two-sided limit does not exist. And on the third--`
**Dr. Brooks:** `Pause.`

### Panel 4: Amara's Turn

Amara stands with a quiet but precise posture. Nia sits lower in her chair,
listening fully.

**Dr. Brooks:** `Amara, would you take the third?`
**Amara:** `The graph grows without bound from one side. That is one-sided behavior, not a function value.`
