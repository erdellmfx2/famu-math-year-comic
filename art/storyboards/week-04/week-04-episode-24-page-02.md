## Panel Plan

### Panel 1: Smooth Repetition

Brief visual montage panel: Malik tells Nia, his parents on a phone screen, and
Dr. Bennett in separate small visual beats that alternate was expected.

**Caption:** `Each repetition became smoother.`

### Panel 2: Outside Porter Center

Simone and Malik stand by the Porter Center railing in late-afternoon light.

**Simone:** `You can be disappointed and still accept the role. Those facts are not mutually exclusive.`

### Panel 3: The Stapler

Malik gives his careful denial; Simone's dry honesty breaks through it.

**Malik:** `I am not disappointed.`
**Simone:** `Then you are doing an incredible impression of somebody swallowing a stapler.`

### Panel 4: Open Review

Simone holds an analytics dashboard device while Malik looks at the open review
notice on his phone.

**Simone:** `Contribution is not the same as auditioning every second you are in the room.`
