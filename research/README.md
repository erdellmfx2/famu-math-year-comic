# Research Source Boundary

This directory preserves source material used to understand academic pacing, extracurricular opportunities, athletics, and the earlier campus concept.

## Use in V2

- Syllabi may inform the order and difficulty of calculus topics.
- Calendars may inform realistic semester rhythm, breaks, exams, and sports cadence.
- Academic-activity research may inspire fictional talks, competitions, lab visits, and presentations.
- Historical campus photographs and building references are provenance only.

## Restrictions

The active story is set at fictional McCall-Hart University in fictional Bellwether, Alabama. Do not reproduce real logos, exact building likenesses, identifying signage, uniforms, proprietary marks, or a real institution's visual identity. Translate useful structural research into original fictional details defined by `story/setting_bible_v2.md`.
