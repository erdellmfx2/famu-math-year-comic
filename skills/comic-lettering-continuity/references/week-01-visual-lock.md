## Measured Style

- Dialogue typeface: Comic Sans MS Regular or the closest exact installed
  match; never substitute a bold geometric UI face.
- Dialogue balloon: white organic oval, 2-3 px near-black outline at the
  Week 1 working resolution, visible mouth-directed tail.
- Narration box: `#FFF4D6` pale cream/yellow fill, 2-3 px near-black outline,
  slightly square corners, no tail.
- Text: `#171717`, centered in balloons and left-aligned in narration boxes
  when space allows.
- Title-card colors: preserve the approved navy, copper, and cream pixels from
  the series-logo base.
- Face clearance: balloon and caption bodies never overlap a character's face.
  When the unlettered art lacks safe negative space, add a pale cream lettering
  band above the panel and proportionally fit the artwork below it.
