# Week 1 Day 2 Video Design

## Style

Create a warm animated-comic video native to *The Formula of Becoming*. The setting is McCall-Hart University's Quantitative Futures Lab on a quiet Saturday morning. Let warm window light, walnut tables, navy clothing, copper accents, and Nia's iced coffee make the room feel alive while the camera motion remains patient and observant.

## Format

- Canvas: 1080 x 1920 vertical
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Delivery: Captioned MP4 with full-cast AI-generated narration and no music
- Captions: White words in a translucent indigo rail; current word highlighted in FAMU dark green `#215732`
- Safe area: Keep captions about 235 px above the bottom edge and preserve the characters' faces, hands, notebook, calculator, laptop, and budget screen.

## Motion

- Title card: Intact, gently held for five seconds.
- Story panels: One continuous 8-10 percent Ken Burns zoom-out with subtle drift and 0.6-second crossfades.
- Message card: Intact, caption-free seven-second hold with a restrained finish fade.

## Restrictions

- Use only approved unlettered Episode 2 comic pages beneath the narration.
- Do not add speech balloons, logos, labels, new art, real-university marks, or unrelated imagery.
- Keep the title and message-card wording uncropped and unaltered.
