# Design: Week 1 Day 4

- Delivery: 1080 by 1920, 30 fps, captioned portrait social video.
- Cards: contain the approved title and thank-you cards intact over a deep indigo matte.
- Story art: use the unlettered pages only. Show one panel at a time over a softly blurred copy of the same page.
- Framing: preserve the active speaker, listener reactions, and the broad Eliza Moss Hall staircase.
- Motion: each story scene begins at 110% scale and settles continuously to 100% with a small one-way drift.
- Captions: white Montserrat on a restrained dark indigo rail; only the spoken word receives a `#215732` backing pill.
- Audio: no music or effects. Use the accepted full-cast voices for narrator, Dr. Brooks, Malik, and Nia.
