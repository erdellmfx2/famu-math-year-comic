# Design: Week 1 Day 3

- Delivery: 1080 by 1920, 30 fps, captioned portrait social video.
- Cards: contain the title and closing cards intact over an indigo matte.
- Story art: preserve full horizontal panels over a softly blurred copy of the same panel; never crop away Malik, Nia, Dr. Brooks, notebook, or laptop.
- Motion: each scene begins at 110% scale and settles to 100% with a minor one-way drift.
- Captions: white Montserrat on a restrained dark indigo rail; only the spoken word receives a `#215732` backing pill.
- Audio: no music or effects. AI-generated full-cast voices only.
