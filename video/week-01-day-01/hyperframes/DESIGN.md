# Week 1 Day 1 Video Design

## Style Prompt

Create a warm, cinematic animated-comic slideshow that feels native to *The Formula of Becoming*. Preserve the approved indigo, copper, cream, honey-limestone, and dark-walnut visual identity. The title and message cards should appear intact. During narration, isolate the appropriate unlettered comic panel as a full-screen vertical image and use a clearly visible, continuous documentary zoom-out to hold attention without competing with the prose.

## Format

- Canvas: 1080 x 1920 vertical
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Delivery: MP4
- Audio: Full-cast prose narration with distinct narrator, Malik, Nia, and Dr. Brooks voices; no background music
- Captions: White active-word captions with the current word highlighted in dark FAMU green
- Safe area: Keep essential faces and props inside the central 900 x 1680 region

## Colors

- Deep indigo background: `#07172E`
- Copper accent: `#C87542`
- Warm cream: `#F1E2C3`
- Limestone neutral: `#D7C7AD`
- Dark walnut: `#2C211B`

## Typography

The approved title and message cards supply all display typography. During narrated scenes, place captions in a dark indigo readability rail near the lower safe area. Keep the title and message cards caption-free.

## Image Treatment

- Use only approved title, unlettered comic, and message-card assets.
- Fill the vertical canvas without stretching.
- Crop from the full comic pages to isolate the active panel.
- Begin each story panel 9-10 percent enlarged and slowly settle toward the full safe crop.
- Add only a slight directional drift when the crop has safe visual room.
- Keep faces, hands, the laptop, calculator, notebook, and classroom board visible when they carry the prose beat.
- Add a very light indigo edge vignette only when a crop reveals unused canvas.

## Motion

- Title card: Gentle 1.5 percent push-in over a five-second hold.
- Panel entrances: Soft copper-tinted crossfade, 0.6 seconds.
- Within panels: One clearly perceptible, continuous Ken Burns zoom-out per prose chapter or panel beat.
- Panel changes: Crossfade while the outgoing panel remains fully visible.
- Message card: Slow push-in, then a clean fade to deep indigo.

## What Not To Do

- Do not show speech bubbles, scene labels, decorative type, or new logos over the comic art.
- Do not use fast zooms, spins, bounce easing, glitch effects, or parallax that distorts faces.
- Do not invent new art, backgrounds, or intermediate character poses.
- Do not use generic purple gradients or modern-tech overlays.
- Do not cut away from a panel before the related prose beat finishes.
