# Week 1 Day 1 Captioned Comparison

## Purpose

Create a separate comparison version of the approved full-cast video. Preserve the original
picture, voice performances, title card, Ken Burns motion, and closing message card exactly.

## Caption Direction

- Place captions near the bottom within the portrait social-media safe area.
- Show measured groups of no more than five words.
- Render all words in white over a restrained dark translucent indigo rail.
- Highlight only the word currently being spoken with FAMU dark green, Pantone 357 C
  represented on screen as `#215732`.
- Use a small scale lift on the active word; avoid bounce, glow, or distracting motion.
- Display no captions during the opening title card or closing message card.

## Delivery

- 1080 by 1920, 30 fps.
- Keep the uncaptioned final unchanged.
- Export as `week-01-day-01-full-cast-captioned-final.mp4`.
